# Label-by-Label Check — Cystex vs. AZO Urinary Tract Defense (2026-11-04)

**Status:** Draft verification file, built under the Research Logic & Evidence Framework (2026-11-04).
It does NOT overwrite the framework file, the Nov 4 ChatGPT Branch import, or any existing work (old-with-new).
No personal experience is interpreted here; this is label comparison only.

## Direct answer

On the current DailyMed labels checked Nov 4, 2026, Cystex and AZO Urinary Tract Defense
list the SAME active ingredients at the SAME strengths per tablet — methenamine 162 mg and
sodium salicylate 162.5 mg — but they are NOT shown to be identical products: labeler, NDC,
sodium-per-tablet, and inactive ingredients differ.

## What the sources say

### Cystex — DailyMed, Clarion Brands, LLC
- Label revised December 2025 (DailyMed result, NDC 69693-518).
- Active ingredients (in each tablet): methenamine 162 mg — Antibacterial;
  sodium salicylate 162.5 mg (NSAID) — Analgesic (pain reliever).
- Uses: temporarily relieves pain, burning, frequency and urgency of urination.
- Each tablet contains: sodium 24.4 mg.
- Ingredient identity: METHENAMINE (UNII: J50OIX95QV); SODIUM SALICYLATE (UNII: WIQ1H85SYP).
- Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=ac0f9a8c-164a-4908-8b1d-bb714a82550d
- Second Cystex listing (repackaged, NDC 69693-513, revised Dec 27, 2025) lists the same actives/strengths:
  https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=4fe33a01-90de-451e-8b54-d84304c842d9&version=4

### AZO Urinary Tract Defense — DailyMed, i-Health, Inc.
- Label updated November 25, 2025 (DailyMed result, NDC 49973-011).
- Active ingredients (in each tablet): methenamine 162 mg — Antibacterial;
  sodium salicylate 162.5 mg (NSAID) — Analgesic (pain reliever).
- Uses: temporarily relieves pain & burning, frequency and urgency of urination.
- Directions (both labels, adults/children 12 years and over): 2 tablets with a full glass of water, 3 times a day.
- Each tablet contains: sodium 25 mg (vs. Cystex 24.4 mg).
- Ingredient identity: same UNIIs — METHENAMINE J50OIX95QV; SODIUM SALICYLATE WIQ1H85SYP.
- DailyMed disclaimer on the AZO listing: "This drug has not been found by FDA to be safe and effective,
  and this labeling has not been approved by FDA." Marketing status on both listings: unapproved drug other.
- Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=ba13a608-e4c5-4465-8be8-be0fd1fd46ae

## What can be concluded

- The provisional match in the Nov 4 ChatGPT share — methenamine 162 mg + sodium salicylate
  162.5 mg per tablet — is CONFIRMED at the listed-active level on both current DailyMed labels.
- The defensible conclusion, per Framework §3/§9, is only: "same listed actives at same strengths."
- Methenamine, methenamine hippurate, and methamphetamine remain three distinct identities;
  neither of these two labels lists hippurate or methamphetamine as an active.

## What cannot yet be concluded

- That the two products are identical overall (inactives, formulation, manufacturing, and
  bioequivalence are not established by matching listed actives alone).
- A complete inactive-by-inactive DailyMed comparison for Cystex (secondary-source list only, so far).
- Anything about any person's experience with either product — that stays in the personal file,
  without interpretation, and is not used as proof here.
