## The combined statement, in his words, completed
Bacterial pathogens rely on diverse capsular polysaccharides (CPS). Major fungal pathogens utilize analogous cell wall and capsular polymers to evade host immunity. By integrating these fungal elements, the repository now spans the continuum of glycoconjugate technologies from nutritional soy polysaccharides to bacterial and fungal virulence armor.

## Postulate 1 — Cryptococcus neoformans / gattii, GXM capsule
**His, as given:** The primary virulence factor is the Glucuronoxylomannan (GXM) capsule, alongside galactoxylomannan (GalXM). Functionally analogous to pneumococcal CPS, experimental GXM-protein conjugates (using CRM197 or TT) are researched to bridge bacterial conjugate architecture with fungal capsular targets.

**Test, three ways:**
1. [D] Capsule composition: GXM and GalXM at ratios of 90% to 10% respectively — Journal of Fungi 2024 review, "Will the Real Immunogens Please Stand Up".
2. [D] GXM-TT: "GXM-based vaccine approaches have focused on conjugating GXM to carrier proteins such as tetanus toxoid (GXM-TT), which induces antibody mediated protection and showed prolonged survival in vaccinated mice after C. neoformans challenge".
3. [D] CRM197: semisynthetic glycoconjugate vaccines with a synthetic decasaccharide M2 motif antigen conjugated to CRM197 (and anthrax PA) "were found to induce protective antibodies against GXM but showed modest protection in murine models"; the ACS study: CRM197 conjugate produced "weakly opsonic antibodies and displayed trends toward increased median survival" (18 vs 15 days, p = 0.06).

**Verdict: HOLDS [H→D], with the honest caveat filed beside it.** The analogy and the conjugate research hold. The limit is explicit in the same review: "Currently, there are no fungal vaccines available for clinical use". Researched, not licensed. *C. gattii* was not separately verified in these three sources — the GXM work cited is *C. neoformans*; gattii inclusion is [GAP] here, not upgraded.

## Postulate 2 — Candida albicans / auris, mannan and beta-glucan
**His, as given:** Features a cell wall rich in mannan and beta-glucan complexes. The outer fibrillar layer consists of phosphopeptidomannan, which acts similarly to bacterial surface antigens in triggering pattern recognition receptors.

**Test, three ways:**
1. [D] Cell wall: "The C. albicans cell wall is a multilayered structure composed mainly of sugars (>80%)" and "The surface is covered by phosphopeptidomannan (commonly named mannan)".
2. [D] Layers: "The intermediate layer of the cell wall is composed of a polysaccharide polymer backbone consisting of β-glucans (both β-1,3, and β-1,6 Dglucopyrannosyls)".
3. [D] PRRs: "mannans and β-glucans are differently recognized by host pattern recognition receptors (PRRs). β-1,3-glucans are recognized by dectin-1, α-mannan is recognized by the mannose receptor, dectin-2, DC-SIGN, mannosebinding lectin (MBL), and langerin".

**Verdict: HOLDS [H→D] for C. albicans.** *C. auris* was not separately verified in these three sources — its inclusion is [GAP] here, not upgraded. The "acts similarly to bacterial surface antigens" phrasing is his functional parallel, filed as [LOGIC] — the PRR recognition itself is documented, the bacterial-surface-antigen parallel is the analogy, not a documented identity.

## Postulate 3 — Aspergillus fumigatus, galactomannan
**His, as given:** Utilizes Galactomannan as a key diagnostic biomarker and cell wall component, representing another high-molecular-weight polysaccharide structure that poses similar clearance challenges to encapsulated bacteria.

**Test, three ways:**
1. [D] Biomarker: "The galactomannan (GM) that is produced by the human fungal pathogen Aspergillus fumigatus is an emblematic biomarker in medical mycology".
2. [D] Composition / forms: GM is "composed of two monosaccharides: mannose and galactofuranose" and has "three different forms: (i) membrane bound through a glycosylphosphatidylinositol (GPI)-anchor, (ii) covalently linked to β-1,3-glucans in the cell wall, or (iii) released in the culture medium as a free polymer".
3. [D] Diagnosis: "The identification of rat monoclonal antibodies against the GM has led to the development of a sensitive diagnositic enzyme immunoassay" and "the GM remains the unavoidable biomarker for the IA diagnosis".

**Verdict: HOLDS [H→D].** The "similar clearance challenges to encapsulated bacteria" part is his functional parallel, filed as [LOGIC] — the biomarker and cell-wall facts are documented, the clearance-challenge parallel is the analogy.

## Simply put
Bacteria wear sugar coats to hide from the immune system. Some fungi wear sugar coats too, in different shapes. Scientists are trying the same trick that worked for bacteria — gluing the sugar coat to a toxoid carrier, like tetanus toxoid or CRM197 — to teach the immune system to see the fungal coat. It works in mice, in early research. No fungal vaccine like that is licensed for people yet.
