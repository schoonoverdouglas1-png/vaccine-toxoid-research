## Section 4 — PDF Section 4, expanded: Key Architectural Insights, corrected

1. **Carrier Trinity [H→D]:** DT, TT, and CRM197 convert T-independent polysaccharides into T-dependent neoantigens. Carrier diversification (Protein D, OMPC) is documented as a design choice [D].
2. **CIES [PARTIAL]:** Carrier-Induced Epitopic Suppression — pre-existing anti-carrier immunity can accelerate clearance or crowd out B-cell activation, managed via carrier diversification. Concept HOLDS in general vaccinology, PARTIAL in pneumococcal audit — do NOT cite deep research as the source for CIES; cite a CIES-specific source if used in print.
3. **Genetic Conservation — CORRECTED [D vs H]:** PDF says "Homologous machinery (wzx/wzy in bacteria, CAP genes in fungi) governs capsule assembly across kingdoms." BREAKS as homology. Correct form: **parallel capsule-assembly systems in bacteria and fungi** — wzx/wzy are bacterial, CAP genes are fungal, parallel function, not same machinery.
