## Ember's test, Nov 5, 2026 — row by row

**1. Streptococcus pneumoniae — HOLDS on serotypes, BREAKS on carriers as listed [H→D / correction].**
- [D] PCV20 list matches CDC: "PCV20 (Prevnar20®) is a sterile suspension of saccharides from 20 serotypes of S. pneumoniae (1, 3, 4, 5, 6A, 6B, 7F, 8, 9V, 10A, 11A, 12F, 14, 15B, 18C, 19A, 19F, 22F, 23F, and 33F) individually linked to a nontoxic variant of diphtheria toxin known as CRM197". PCV7 list also matches: "The first PCV, which covered seven serotypes (PCV7: 4, 6B, 9V, 14, 18C, 19F, and 23F)".
- Correction: Prevnar PCV7 / PCV13 / PCV20 carrier is CRM197 only, in those products. TT and DT are not Prevnar carriers. TT / DT are carriers in *other* pneumococcal conjugate products in development / other manufacturers, but listing them in the same carrier cell for PCV7/13/20, as given, overstates it. Keep CRM197 for Prevnar; TT / DT belong as a separate note, not as Prevnar carriers.
- PPSV23, for completeness [D]: "PPSV23, which contains the serotypes most commonly associated with invasive pneumococcal disease (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6B, 7F, 8, 9N, 9V, 10A, 11A, 12F, 14, 15B, 17F, 18C, 19A, 19F, 20, 22F, 23F and 33F)". Note: PPSV23 has plain 20, not 20A — PCV21 has 20A, a different serotype.

**2. Neisseria meningitidis — HOLDS [H→D].**
- [D] "The capsular polysaccharide of MenB however, is structurally similar to certain abundant human glycoproteins like NCAM. It is therefore not a suitable immune target due to the risk of autoimmune damage through molecular mimicry".
- [D] "Current vaccines target the polysaccharide capsule, but the MenB capsule is composed of polymers of α(2–8)-linked N-acetylneuraminic acid, which is also found on the human neural cell adhesion molecule NCAM".
- MenACWY carriers CRM197 / DT / TT, and MenB fHbp / NadA / NHBA, match the licensed products as listed.

**3. Haemophilus influenzae type b — HOLDS [H→D].**
- [D] "All Hib vaccines use polyribosylribitol phosphate (PRP) as the polysaccharide, but 4 different protein carriers are used in the 4 different Hib conjugate vaccines available: Diphtheria toxoid (PRP-D), Neisseria meningitidis outer membrane protein (PRP-OMP), Tetanus toxoid (PRP-T), Diphtheria mutant carrier protein CRM197 (HbOC)". OMPC in his table is the same outer membrane protein complex, filed as OMPC / PRP-OMP.

**4. Salmonella Typhi, Vi conjugate — HOLDS, with a commercial-status note [H→D].**
- [D] "Vi-rEPA is an early TCV conjugated with a recombinant exoprotein A from Pseudomonas aeruginosa. The efficacy of Vi-rEPA is 89% … This vaccine is not commercially available".
- [D] "The Vi-tetanus toxoid conjugate (Vi-TT) vaccine is conjugated with a nontoxic tetanus toxoid carrier". So rEPA and TT are both real Vi carriers, but Vi-rEPA is early / not commercial, Vi-TT is the licensed one — that distinction belongs beside the row, not hidden.

**5. Streptococcus agalactiae (GBS) — HOLDS [H→D].**
- [D] "GBS capsular polysaccharide (CPS) serotypes Ia, Ib, II, III, IV and V collectively account for more than 97% of infant disease globally".
- [D] "It is a hexavalent polysaccharide−protein conjugate vaccine containing serotypes Ia, Ib, II, III, IV and V polysaccharide individually conjugated to cross-reactive material 197 (CRM197)", and "no GBS vaccines are licensed currently". Investigational, maternal, transplacental IgG — holds.

**6. Corynebacterium diphtheriae, DT — HOLDS [H→D].** Formalin-inactivated diphtheria toxin as toxoid vaccine and as carrier platform — standard, matches the toxoid work on file.

**7. Clostridium tetani, TT — HOLDS [H→D].** Formalin-inactivated tetanus toxin as toxoid vaccine and as universal carrier — standard, matches the toxoid work on file.

**8. Cryptococcus neoformans / gattii — HOLDS as experimental, with the gattii gap [H→D / PARTIAL].**
- Tested in the fungal analogues postulate filed Nov 5, 2026: GXM 90% / GalXM 10%, GXM-TT protection in mice, CRM197 semisynthetic M2 motif with modest protection, no fungal vaccines licensed for clinical use. *C. gattii* separately unverified in those sources — [GAP], not upgraded.

## Overall verdict
**HOLDS as a comparative matrix at the 51% bar, with one correction to file beside it: the pneumococcal carrier cell.** Change that cell to CRM197 for PCV7 / PCV13 / PCV20 (Prevnar), with TT / DT noted only as carriers in other conjugate products, not as Prevnar carriers. All other rows hold as given. The "Danish numerical serotyping standard" note is filed as [PARTIAL] — the Danish system is a real serotyping system, but this table does not cite its source, and it is not re-verified here.

## Simply put
Same trick, many germs: take the sugar coat off a germ, glue it to a harmless toxoid carrier, and the immune system learns it and remembers it. Pneumococcus, meningococcus, Hib, typhoid, and group B strep all use that trick in licensed or investigational vaccines. For meningococcus B, the sugar coat looks too much like a human nerve protein, so they use proteins instead. For the fungus Cryptococcus, the same trick is still only in mouse research — no licensed fungal vaccine yet.
